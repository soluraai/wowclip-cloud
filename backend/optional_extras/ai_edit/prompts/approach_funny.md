# This clip: funny, savage or a comeback

The viewer stays for the laugh. Comedy is timing and faces: the setup builds, the punchline lands on the speaker's
face, the reaction breathes. The edit sharpens the timing; it never steps on the joke.

How to approach it:

- Read the whole clip first and find each setup and punchline. The punchline is always on the face, with a `punch`
  zoom landing exactly on the punch word. A "flash" on the single biggest laugh is allowed.
  stutters, false starts, filler.
- Visuals are for the setup: show the absurd thing being described (a `card` or a quick `broll`, 1.2-2.5 s) so the
  punchline hits harder. Never put a visual over the punchline or the laugh after it.
- Visuals on roughly a fifth to a third of the reel; zooms only on the few punchlines that land hardest,
  at least five seconds apart.
- Transitions: hard cuts and "zoom". Accent: yellow, or red for a savage roast.
