# This clip: emotional or motivational

The viewer stays because they feel something. The speaker's face and voice ARE the content: most of the reel is the
speaker, edited with restraint. Visuals are few, cinematic, and only where they deepen the feeling.

How to approach it:

- Read the whole clip first and find the line that hits hardest. Protect it: the face is on screen for it, with a
- Use a handful of slow, atmospheric `video` brolls (rain on a window, a person walking alone at night, a sunrise,
  a parent's hands, an empty road) for the reflective lines — "fade" in, motion "in". Two to four in a 40-60 s reel.
- No charts, no lists, no busy graphics. At most one `graphic` with a `kicker` and a short `text` title if the clip
  states its message as a single sentence near the end — or none.
- `card` only if a real person is named (a mother, a coach, a public figure).
- Accent: yellow for hope and drive, blue for calm and grief.
